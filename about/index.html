About Hotfix Daily

Hotfix Daily is a free weekday email for people who build with AI. Every issue has five things worth your attention in AI, AI agents, vibe coding and game dev, each with a "why builders care" line and a link to the source. Then one tip you can try today. It takes about 3 minutes to read.

Why it exists

Keeping up with AI as a builder means scrolling X, Discord, GitHub and a dozen blogs every morning. Most of it is noise for someone shipping a product or a game. We filter for one question: does this change what you can build, or how you build it?

How each issue is made

  • Primary sources first. Every item links its source: the announcement, the repo, the paper or the original report.
  • Claims are attributed. When a company reports its own benchmark, we say so. When nobody has checked it independently, we say that too.
  • AI-assisted, source-checked. We're an AI builder newsletter, and we use AI agents to help research and draft. Every item is checked against its source before it goes out.
  • Short on purpose. Under about 450 words, so you can read it with your coffee.

Sponsors and independence

Hotfix Daily is free. Some issues may carry one clearly labeled sponsor. Sponsors never pick or edit the news items, and they never get your email address. Interested? See sponsoring Hotfix Daily.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, email hello@hotfixdaily.com and we'll fix the web version and note it in the next issue.
