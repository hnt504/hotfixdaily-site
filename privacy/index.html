Privacy policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

What we collect

When you subscribe, we collect your email address. Our email provider, Kit, stores it and sends the newsletter. Kit may record standard email metrics such as opens and clicks, and basic technical data like IP address and signup time.

How we use it

Only to send you Hotfix Daily and occasional messages about the newsletter. We don't sell, rent, or share your email address with anyone except the providers needed to run the newsletter.

Sponsors

Some issues may include a clearly labeled sponsor. Sponsors never receive your email address or personal data.

This website

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly, cookieless visitor counter that collects no personal data. This site uses no ad trackers or cookies of its own. The signup form sends your email directly to Kit.

Affiliate links

Some guides contain affiliate links, marked "(affiliate)". If you sign up through one, Hotfix Daily may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It never affects what we recommend, and the affiliate programs don't get your email address.

Your choices

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. To have your data deleted, or for any privacy question, email hello@hotfixdaily.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.
